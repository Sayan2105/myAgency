Questions we hear

Straight answers about enquiries, CRM and automation.

How can a travel agency stop losing Instagram and WhatsApp enquiries?

Bring every chat into one place. We connect Instagram and WhatsApp to a custom CRM, so each enquiry becomes a lead with a status, an owner and a follow-up reminder. Nothing sits unread in a DM, and you can see who still needs a reply.

Can a CRM send me a daily business summary on Telegram or WhatsApp?

Yes. We connect your CRM to a Telegram or WhatsApp bot that sends one short message a day: new enquiries, quotes waiting for a reply and bookings confirmed. You see the day at a glance without opening any software.

Do you only work with travel agencies?

No. Travel agencies are our first speciality, but the same setup suits any business that takes orders or enquiries through chats, like coaching centres, salons and online shops. We also build websites and ERP software.

What do you need from us to get started?

A short call about how enquiries reach you today, access to your Instagram or WhatsApp business account, and a list of what you want in your daily summary. WhatsApp and Instagram connections need Meta's business approval, and we guide you through it.
